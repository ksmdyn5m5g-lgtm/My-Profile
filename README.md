## Internship at Fraunhofer IEM

How do you know the suitcase arriving at the end of a conveyor is the same one that went in a minute earlier?

That's one of the problems I worked on during my internship at Fraunhofer IEM, as part of an automated luggage handling system. I focused on two areas:

**Validation**
Making sure each piece of luggage is correctly recognised as it moves through the system, and that the team is alerted when something doesn't add up.

**HMI and digital twin**
A live dashboard that gives the team a clear picture of the whole line at a glance, including a digital twin that follows the luggage on the belt in real time.

Along the way I also supported the work on measuring luggage dimensions. I finished by documenting everything and handing it over to the team.

A big thank you to my supervisor, Dr. Yee Jingye, for the guidance and trust throughout the internship, and to Dr. Low Cheng Yee and Dr. Ram for helping me through the internship process. Thank you as well to everyone at Fraunhofer IEM for the feedback and the chance to work on a real system.

## High Five – Learning Guide Heilbronn

A learning guide built at the Future City Hackathon 2026 of Hochschule Heilbronn, for the challenge set by aim. It brings the AI, coding and gaming offers in Heilbronn together in one place:

- **Parents** search offers with filters and a map, and approve their child's requests.
- **Children** explore a mini Heilbronn and ask their parents to sign them up.
- **Institutions** add their offers.
